# Flower Shop Online Ordering System - PM & BA Case Study

A project management and business analysis case study for moving a flower shop from manual phone and WhatsApp orders to an online ordering system.

**Author:** Kalani Tharanga - Cybersecurity Undergraduate, Edith Cowan University
**Focus:** Requirements analysis · Agile project planning · Security-aware design

---

## 📌 The Business Problem
Many small flower shops in Sri Lanka still take orders by phone, WhatsApp and walk-in, and record them by hand. During peak seasons such as Valentine's Day and Mother's Day, this leads to missed calls, order mistakes and lost sales, and customers have no way to track their delivery.

This case study plans an online ordering system to solve these problems, drawing on my experience as a Junior Sales Executive in the flower industry.
